# ISMS scope statement

Receipters Technologies

| | |
|---|---|
| Document | Information Security Management System (ISMS) scope, ISO/IEC 27001 |
| Owner | Gordon Odira, Founder |
| Date | 20 November 2026 |
| Review | Every year, or when the systems in scope change |

## Scope

The information security management system covers the design, development, hosting and support of:

- the Receipters website (www.receipters.com) and the owners console;
- the products ChomaPOS, ChomaPOS Plus, NairoPOS, BodaPOS, LinearPOS, FursaPay and ShahidiBox.

It includes the cloud services that run them, the personal and business data they process, the people who develop, administer or support them, and the Kifaru Place office, South B, Nairobi, together with remote work by those people.

## Out of scope

- DukaPOS (dukapos.com).
- Customers' own systems.
- Services that Receipters does not control.

## Interested parties and requirements

- Customers and users.
- The Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (Data Protection Act, 2019).
- Payment and banking partners.
- Cloud providers and their terms.

This repository is public. Keep only this scope statement and other documents that are safe to publish here. Do not add the risk register, asset list with admin access, incident records or any credentials. Those belong in a private location.
